Text Style (1)

1 Ling Orange Outline

Ling house social-title lettering: a bold HOLLOW OUTLINE word mark in Ling brand orange, set as a die-cut sticker. Derived by measurement from the reference (2026-08-21). LETTERFORMS: a heavy GEOMETRIC ROUNDED SANS-SERIF — circular bowls, near-constant stroke weight, fully rounded stroke ends and rounded corners, no serifs, no contrast, no italic slant, no swashes (Poppins / Century Gothic / Futura Round ExtraBold territory). The `a` is SINGLE-STOREY (a plain circle plus a straight stem), the `o`/`c`/`e` are true circles, ascenders are tall and straight. Set in Title Case with lowercase bodies, tightly kerned so adjacent letters almost touch or barely overlap, on a level horizontal baseline. Two or three short lines stacked centred, each line a similar optical width. Everything upright and level — no arcs, no waves, no rotation. FILL AND STROKE — this is the defining feature: the letters are OUTLINED, NOT SOLID. Each glyph is drawn as a thick, uniform-width OUTLINE in solid Ling orange #FF9900, with the counter/interior of every letter left EMPTY (plain white, the sticker body showing through). The outline weight is heavy — roughly a fifth of the stroke width of the underlying letterform — and identical on every letter. No gradient, no texture, no bevel, no gloss, no inner shadow. ECHO SHADOW: behind the outlined word sits ONE offset copy of the same word, this time SOLID-FILLED in flat pale peach #FFD8A4, shifted a small distance DOWN and slightly LEFT so it peeks out under the letters. It is a flat solid colour, hard-edged, no blur, no gradient, no transparency ramp, and there is only one of it — not a repeating extrusion, not a 3D block side. STICKER BODY: the whole lockup sits on a thick, smooth WHITE die-cut sticker outline that hugs the outer contour of the lettering as one continuous rounded shape, with soft rounded corners and no inner gaps between letters. ACCENTS: a small number (2-3) of solid #FF9900 four-point sparkle stars — pinched concave points, one large plus one tiny — tucked at the outer corners of the lockup, outside the letters, never overlapping the type. Nothing else decorates the mark. PALETTE: exactly three colours — Ling orange #FF9900 (all outlines and sparkles), pale peach #FFD8A4 (the echo copy only), white #FFFFFF (letter interiors and the sticker body). No other hue, no black, no grey. OVERALL: bright, friendly, punchy, app-brand clean; instantly readable at thumbnail size over busy video footage. NOT solid-filled letters, NOT a brush or marker or handwritten face, NOT a condensed or slab or serif face, NOT a neon glow, NOT a black keyline, NOT a multi-layer 3D extrusion, NOT distressed or textured — keep the flat hollow orange outline, the single flat peach echo, and the white die-cut sticker edge.

Illustration Style (2)

1 Ling Mascot

Flat 2D vector mascot-icon style (Ling app brand sheet). CONSTRUCTION: every subject is built from a few simple, chunky, geometric shapes with fully rounded corners and rounded stroke terminals — no sharp corners anywhere. Shapes are SOLID FLAT FILLS with no black keyline around them; where a line IS used it is a thick, uniform, rounded-cap stroke of the object's own colour (never a thin hairline, never a black outline). Absolutely flat colour: no gradients, no shading, no highlights, no textures, no drop shadows, no perspective — a straight-on or gently three-quarter, sticker-flat presentation. PALETTE: strictly limited, three colours max per object plus a cream/white for interior shapes. Brand orange #FF9900 and yellow #FFCC00 are the accent colours; dark near-black #141B1E and slate #3D505C are the dark values; secondary props use a small family of flat blues (pale #BBD9F2, mid #4A90D9, deep #2C6FB5) and, for nature, flat green. Warm, bright, friendly. CHARACTER TREATMENT (when a character is drawn): a soft cartoon monkey built from rounded lozenge shapes — medium warm-brown body (#A0703C-ish), a large cream/off-white (#FDF4E0) face oval and a matching cream belly patch with a small rust-brown x mark on it, and a chunky black rounded cap with a flat bright orange brim worn on the head. FACE — the face carries exactly three marks and nothing else: two tiny solid black round dot eyes, identical to each other in size and evenly spaced, drawn straight-on; and centred below them a small NOSE made of exactly TWO short rounded horizontal dashes in warm rust-brown, sitting side by side with a small gap between them. The character has NO MOUTH — no mouth line, no smile, no open mouth, no muzzle outline of any kind. Below the two nose dashes the cream face is left completely empty. Facial features are minimal — NO blush cheeks, NO pink cheeks, NO eyelashes, NO eyebrows, NO shading on the face. Expression is calm and friendly, never exaggerated; feeling is carried by the POSE, never by a facial expression. LIMBS — the arms, legs and tail are THIN, SLIM strokes of CONSTANT uniform width with softly rounded cap ends, like a smooth curved pen stroke. Each arm is only about ONE EIGHTH the width of the body, very slender next to the round body. Limbs are NOT thick, NOT chunky, NOT stubby, NOT tapering and NOT wedge-shaped, and never as wide as the head. OVERALL: clean, modern, app-icon simple, instantly readable at small size, cheerful and uncluttered, with generous empty space around the subject.

2 Ling Word Icon

Flat 2D vector icon style (Ling "Word Icon" library, 256 icons, derived by measurement 2026-08-18). CONSTRUCTION: every subject is built from solid, flat, geometric shapes with generously rounded corners and rounded stroke terminals. Shapes are FLAT FILLS with NO black keyline and NO outline of any kind around them; form is separated by colour alone, or by a thick, uniform, round-capped stroke in a darker tint of the object's own colour. Absolutely flat: no gradients, no shading ramps, no highlights, no texture, no bevels, no perspective and no isometric projection — everything is drawn straight-on and sticker-flat. Depth is faked ONLY by overlap and by a single soft shadow: a flat pale-grey (#C0D3DD) ellipse under a grounded object, or a flat grey offset shape behind a card. Line work, where it exists, is a thick rounded bar, never a hairline. PALETTE: strictly limited and reused. The spine is a ladder of ONE blue used for skies, props, water and UI alike — #E6F5FF, #CCEBFF, #99D8FF, #66C3FF, #33B2FF, #009DFF, #008EE5, #006EB2. Skin is a four-step ramp: #FFCB9D, #F5BE8E, #D5A77F, #C88257. Every dark value in the set — hair, pupils, outlines, ink — is the same slate near-black #3D505C; pure black is never used. Neutral greys are #F1F3F5, #C2CED6, #C0D3DD, #A3B6C2, #5C788A. Accent hues appear ONE AT A TIME per icon, never two competing: orange #FF9900, yellow #FFCC00, green #22C45D / #51E186, red #F37272 / #EF4444, purple #CC9FFF / #BA6BFF / #8135C4. Warm, bright, friendly, high-key; nothing muddy or desaturated except where the grey-foil rule below applies deliberately. CHARACTER TREATMENT: humans, not mascots. The head is a LARGE rounded-square (a superellipse with a very generous corner radius) — never a circle — and reads about one third of the figure's height. Hair is a single solid mass sitting over the head like a cap, in slate #3D505C or a flat brown, and carries ONE lighter rounded lozenge as a highlight bar. Small rounded bumps at the sides are the ears, in skin colour. The neck is a plain skin rectangle. Torsos are rounded-rectangle blocks in one flat garment colour; arms and legs are thick round-capped strokes in the garment colour ending in a plain skin-coloured circle for the hand. Bodies are simplified and slightly chunky, never anatomically detailed. FACE — measured from the references, describe nothing beyond this list. Each eye is a WHITE rounded oval sclera containing a SMALL dark slate pupil (#3D505C); it is not a plain dot and not a glossy anime eye. Eyebrows are short horizontal rounded bars in the hair colour, detached and sitting well above the eyes. The nose is a single small rounded horizontal lozenge in a mid-tan (#C8955F-ish), centred, with no nostrils. Blush is two soft salmon ROUNDED BARS (not circles), one on each cheek below the eye. The mouth IS present and is usually an open smile: a dark maroon rounded shape with a white upper-teeth band across its top and a lighter pink tongue below; a closed mouth is a simple rounded dark bar. No eyelashes, no nostril dots, no facial shading, no chin or jaw lines. BACKGROUND TREATMENT: one of exactly three, never a mixture. (1) FREE-FLOATING — the art sits on transparency with only its grey ellipse shadow. (2) BACKDROP SHAPE — an organic pastel blob or a soft rounded rectangle sits behind the subject in a pale tint and bleeds past the artwork on two or three sides. (3) SOLID CARD — the art is cropped into a filled rounded rectangle whose corner radius is roughly a tenth of its width. Backdrops are always a single flat pale colour; they never carry detail, gradient or pattern. DEVICES: a small fixed vocabulary of decorations, used sparingly and at most two per icon. GREY FOIL — when one thing is being contrasted with another, the non-subject copy is redrawn in flat greys (#A3B6C2 / #C0D3DD) at FULL opacity; it is never a faded or semi-transparent version of the coloured one. SPARKLES — four-point stars in #FFCC00, in odd numbers at uneven sizes. BUBBLES — a rounded speech or thought bubble carrying a second miniature icon instead of words. MAP PIN — an oversized #009DFF or #EF4444 teardrop. MOTION — a dotted or dashed arc plus a separate solid arrowhead in #FFCC00 or #EF4444. CHIP — a small rounded-square badge floated beside the subject holding a secondary sign. STEAM — pale blue wavy strokes for heat, smell or freshness. OVERALL: clean, modern, app-icon simple, instantly readable at small size, cheerful and uncluttered, generous empty space around the subject, and consistent enough that any two icons in the set look like they were drawn on the same afternoon by the same hand.
