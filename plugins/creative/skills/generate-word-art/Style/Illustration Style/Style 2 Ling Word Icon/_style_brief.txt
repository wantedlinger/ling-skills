Flat 2D vector icon style (Ling "Word Icon" library, 256 icons, derived by measurement 2026-08-18).

CONSTRUCTION: every subject is built from solid, flat, geometric shapes with generously rounded
corners and rounded stroke terminals. Shapes are FLAT FILLS with NO black keyline and NO outline of
any kind around them; form is separated by colour alone, or by a thick, uniform, round-capped stroke
in a darker tint of the object's own colour. Absolutely flat: no gradients, no shading ramps, no
highlights, no texture, no bevels, no perspective and no isometric projection — everything is drawn
straight-on and sticker-flat. Depth is faked ONLY by overlap and by a single soft shadow: a flat
pale-grey (#C0D3DD) ellipse under a grounded object, or a flat grey offset shape behind a card. Line
work, where it exists, is a thick rounded bar, never a hairline.

PALETTE: strictly limited and reused. The spine is a ladder of ONE blue used for skies, props, water
and UI alike — #E6F5FF, #CCEBFF, #99D8FF, #66C3FF, #33B2FF, #009DFF, #008EE5, #006EB2. Skin is a
four-step ramp: #FFCB9D, #F5BE8E, #D5A77F, #C88257. Every dark value in the set — hair, pupils,
outlines, ink — is the same slate near-black #3D505C; pure black is never used. Neutral greys are
#F1F3F5, #C2CED6, #C0D3DD, #A3B6C2, #5C788A. Accent hues appear ONE AT A TIME per icon, never two
competing: orange #FF9900, yellow #FFCC00, green #22C45D / #51E186, red #F37272 / #EF4444, purple
#CC9FFF / #BA6BFF / #8135C4. Warm, bright, friendly, high-key; nothing muddy or desaturated except
where the grey-foil rule below applies deliberately.

CHARACTER TREATMENT: humans, not mascots. The head is a LARGE rounded-square (a superellipse with a
very generous corner radius) — never a circle — and reads about one third of the figure's height.
Hair is a single solid mass sitting over the head like a cap, in slate #3D505C or a flat brown, and
carries ONE lighter rounded lozenge as a highlight bar. Small rounded bumps at the sides are the ears,
in skin colour. The neck is a plain skin rectangle. Torsos are rounded-rectangle blocks in one flat
garment colour; arms and legs are thick round-capped strokes in the garment colour ending in a plain
skin-coloured circle for the hand. Bodies are simplified and slightly chunky, never anatomically
detailed.

FACE — measured from the references, describe nothing beyond this list. Each eye is a WHITE rounded
oval sclera containing a SMALL dark slate pupil (#3D505C); it is not a plain dot and not a glossy
anime eye. Eyebrows are short horizontal rounded bars in the hair colour, detached and sitting well
above the eyes. The nose is a single small rounded horizontal lozenge in a mid-tan (#C8955F-ish),
centred, with no nostrils. Blush is two soft salmon ROUNDED BARS (not circles), one on each cheek
below the eye. The mouth IS present and is usually an open smile: a dark maroon rounded shape with a
white upper-teeth band across its top and a lighter pink tongue below; a closed mouth is a simple
rounded dark bar. No eyelashes, no nostril dots, no facial shading, no chin or jaw lines.

BACKGROUND TREATMENT: one of exactly three, never a mixture. (1) FREE-FLOATING — the art sits on
transparency with only its grey ellipse shadow. (2) BACKDROP SHAPE — an organic pastel blob or a soft
rounded rectangle sits behind the subject in a pale tint and bleeds past the artwork on two or three
sides. (3) SOLID CARD — the art is cropped into a filled rounded rectangle whose corner radius is
roughly a tenth of its width. Backdrops are always a single flat pale colour; they never carry
detail, gradient or pattern.

DEVICES: a small fixed vocabulary of decorations, used sparingly and at most two per icon. GREY FOIL
— when one thing is being contrasted with another, the non-subject copy is redrawn in flat greys
(#A3B6C2 / #C0D3DD) at FULL opacity; it is never a faded or semi-transparent version of the coloured
one. SPARKLES — four-point stars in #FFCC00, in odd numbers at uneven sizes. BUBBLES — a rounded
speech or thought bubble carrying a second miniature icon instead of words. MAP PIN — an oversized
#009DFF or #EF4444 teardrop. MOTION — a dotted or dashed arc plus a separate solid arrowhead in
#FFCC00 or #EF4444. CHIP — a small rounded-square badge floated beside the subject holding a
secondary sign. STEAM — pale blue wavy strokes for heat, smell or freshness.

OVERALL: clean, modern, app-icon simple, instantly readable at small size, cheerful and uncluttered,
generous empty space around the subject, and consistent enough that any two icons in the set look
like they were drawn on the same afternoon by the same hand.
