Flat 2D vector mascot-icon style (Ling app brand sheet).

CONSTRUCTION: every subject is built from a few simple, chunky, geometric shapes with fully
rounded corners and rounded stroke terminals — no sharp corners anywhere. Shapes are SOLID
FLAT FILLS with no black keyline around them; where a line IS used it is a thick, uniform,
rounded-cap stroke of the object's own colour (never a thin hairline, never a black outline).
Absolutely flat colour: no gradients, no shading, no highlights, no textures, no drop shadows,
no perspective — a straight-on or gently three-quarter, sticker-flat presentation.

PALETTE: strictly limited, three colours max per object plus a cream/white for interior shapes.
Brand orange #FF9900 and yellow #FFCC00 are the accent colours; dark near-black #141B1E and
slate #3D505C are the dark values; secondary props use a small family of flat blues (pale
#BBD9F2, mid #4A90D9, deep #2C6FB5) and, for nature, flat green. Warm, bright, friendly.

CHARACTER TREATMENT (when a character is drawn): a soft cartoon monkey built from rounded
lozenge shapes — medium warm-brown body (#A0703C-ish), a large cream/off-white (#FDF4E0) face
oval and a matching cream belly patch with a small rust-brown x mark on it, and a chunky black
rounded cap with a flat bright orange brim worn on the head.

FACE — the face carries exactly three marks and nothing else: two tiny solid black round dot
eyes, identical to each other in size and evenly spaced, drawn straight-on; and centred below
them a small NOSE made of exactly TWO short rounded horizontal dashes in warm rust-brown,
sitting side by side with a small gap between them. The character has NO MOUTH — no mouth
line, no smile, no open mouth, no muzzle outline of any kind. Below the two nose dashes the
cream face is left completely empty. Facial features are minimal — NO blush cheeks, NO pink
cheeks, NO eyelashes, NO eyebrows, NO shading on the face. Expression is calm and friendly,
never exaggerated; feeling is carried by the POSE, never by a facial expression.

LIMBS — the arms, legs and tail are THIN, SLIM strokes of CONSTANT uniform width with softly
rounded cap ends, like a smooth curved pen stroke. Each arm is only about ONE EIGHTH the width
of the body, very slender next to the round body. Limbs are NOT thick, NOT chunky, NOT stubby,
NOT tapering and NOT wedge-shaped, and never as wide as the head.

OVERALL: clean, modern, app-icon simple, instantly readable at small size, cheerful and
uncluttered, with generous empty space around the subject.
